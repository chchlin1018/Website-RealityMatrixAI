For the AI era

AI agents need engineering data they can trust. ZXBuilder delivers BIM ↔ OpenUSD data that carries its identity.

Each component leaves Revit with a fixed identity and its connection points, written as plain properties that any OpenUSD program can read without asking Revit. On the way back, it is rebuilt by that identity as a native, editable Revit element with its parameters.

The problem
Design → Build → Operate: models change hands again and again

A fab or an AI data center is handed from owner to designer, contractor and equipment supplier. Each handover is a Revit or CAD file exchange, and the identity of each component is hard to carry through.
